# Anchor validated

ShellBench v0.1: the thesis building as a validated anchor, with its quantities and A1-A3 embodied carbon.

**Contents**
- `docs/anchor_report.md`: what the anchor reproduces, what it does not, and why. Every number traces to a file.
- `src/shellbench/anchor_frame.py`, `anchor_wind.py`, `seismic.py`, `rsa.py`, `anchor_3d.py`: the thesis frame under gravity, wind and earthquake (base-shear and response-spectrum methods), and whole-building periods.
- `src/shellbench/checks.py`: GB 50010 beam flexure and column axial-ratio and minimum-steel checks.
- `src/shellbench/carbon.py`: quantity take-off and A1-A3 carbon (GB/T 51366-2019 factors; 128.2 kgCO2e/m2 grid plan, thesis steel).
- `src/shellbench/calculix.py`, `formfind.py`: CalculiX smoke test and force density form-finding for the shell floor (Track B, floor-r1).
- `docs/anchor_notes.md`, `validation/thesis_values/`: every difference above 10 % explained; thesis flags D1-D29.

Screening-level, elastic, code-level analysis under the stated assumptions. No claim of seismic safety.

Tests: pytest, 132 passed (133 with CalculiX). Python 3.12, OpenSeesPy 3.8.0.0. Licence: MIT.
