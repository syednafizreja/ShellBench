Rule agreed today: the gap matrix uses only papers published 2020-2026. Older papers are kept in a short "Foundations" list and cited for method only.

How to check each paper: click the DOI link and confirm the title, authors, year and journal match the row. The "Free full text" link is the legal open copy I read from (publisher open access or the authors' university repository). No pirate sites were used.

"Read?" says how much I actually read: **Full** = whole text; **Abstract** = abstract only, so nothing beyond the abstract goes in the matrix; **No** = not yet opened [?].

## Access statement (for the Methods section)

All 23 papers in the list, and both foundation papers, are legally free to read without an institutional login: each is open access at the publisher (gold or hybrid OA under a Creative Commons licence) or has the authors' accepted manuscript in a university repository. They were identified with OpenAlex / Unpaywall OA flags and checked on 22 Oct 2026. Where we read an accepted manuscript (rows 1, 2 and 20, and Mergos 2024), we cite the version of record and note that the accepted manuscript was read; content is the same after peer review, but page layout differs. No paywalled paper is used for any claim. Suggested Methods wording [J]: "All sources were accessed through open-access versions of record or authors' accepted manuscripts deposited in institutional repositories."
